Robot kits

Kits are issued in week one and returned in week twelve. The 20 KD deposit comes back when the kit comes back complete. The first replacement of any part is included; the prices below are what a second one costs.

Kit 01 · SPARK

"Tread"

Tracked chassis frame, 2 × 6V gear motors, motor driver board, 2 × reflectance sensors, 1 × ultrasonic sensor, 4 × AA battery holder, 40-piece screw and bracket set, hex driver.

Coding: block editor · Soldering: none

Replacements — gear motor 3 KD · reflectance sensor 2 KD · chassis frame 8 KD

Kit 02 · RELAY

"Relay Board"

Aluminium four-wheel chassis, 4 × gear motors, dual motor driver, solderable sensor shield (supplied unpopulated), 4 × reflectance sensors, 1 × ultrasonic sensor, 6-axis gyro, breadboard, jumper set, 2 × 18650 cells and holder, multimeter.

Coding: text editor · Soldering: yes, at Bench B, supervised

Replacements — sensor shield 6 KD · gyro 5 KD · motor driver 4 KD · 18650 cell 3 KD

Kit 03 · APEX

"Apex Arm"

Three-wheel omni base, 3 × encoder motors, custom soldered sensor board, servo gripper with 2 × metal-gear servos, camera module, single-board computer, 3S battery and charger, spare servo horns and drive belts.

Coding: full control loop, on-device vision · Soldering: yes

Replacements — metal-gear servo 6 KD · camera module 9 KD · encoder motor 7 KD · drive belt 1 KD

Kit 04 · Shared bench kit

"Kite"

3-inch indoor quadcopter, prop guards fitted at all times, flight controller, 4 × spare prop sets, 3 × 450 mAh packs, charger, controller.

Not issued to students and never leaves the Flight Bay. Signed out per session, signed back in before the session ends. No deposit.

Competition track

Format

A 2.4 by 1.2 metre mat, six scored objectives, one 150-second round, two attempts per team with the better attempt counting. Fully autonomous: once the round starts, nobody touches the robot.

Teams

Two or three students. One person is nominated to place the robot and start the round; everyone else stays behind the line. Teams are fixed for the term.

Eligibility

Open to APEX. RELAY students can be invited by their coach from week six.

Scoring — maximum 105 points.
ObjectivePoints
Robot leaves the start square under its own control5
Both colour blocks collected15
Block delivered to the matching zone25 each
Line maintained through the crossing10
Robot returns fully inside the start square15
Round finished under 120 seconds10 bonus

Penalties

−5 for touching the robot after the start signal. The round ends at the third touch, and the score stands at whatever was banked.

Dry runs

Wednesday 18:00–19:30, Mat Room, in weeks 6, 9 and 11. The mat is set to the same layout every time, so a score in week six and a score in week eleven mean the same thing.

Affiliation. This format is ours. We are not affiliated with any competition organiser, we use no organiser's name, mat or rulebook, and we do not register students for external events. Families who want to enter something outside CIRCUIT are welcome to, and we will help them prepare — that is as far as it goes.

Bench rules

Seven rules. They are not negotiable and they apply to coaches too.

  1. 1. Safety glasses at Bench B whenever an iron is hot. No exceptions.
  2. 2. Fume extraction on before the iron is on, off after the iron is cold.
  3. 3. SPARK students do not use the iron. They wire; a coach solders.
  4. 4. Batteries charge on the charge bench only. Never in a bag, never unattended, never overnight.
  5. 5. Props go on the drone in the Flight Bay and come off before it leaves.
  6. 6. The Flight Bay net stays closed while any motor is armed.
  7. 7. Kit boxes are checked in and out every session. A missing part is reported the same day, not found at the end of term.

This list is written in the same plain language on the wall at each bench, and it is read aloud in week one of every term.

Term calendar

Twelve weeks, by week number rather than by date, because a term can start on any Saturday.

WK 1–3 · Stage 1 Build

Unit 01 Build & Drive · Bench A/B/C. Drivetrain built, taken apart, rebuilt. Distance per motor rotation measured and written in the logbook.

WK 4–5 · Stage 2 Control

Unit 02 Circuits & Soldering · Bench B. Sensor board wired, soldered by RELAY and APEX, tested with a multimeter before anything is powered.

WK 6–8 · Stage 2 Control

Unit 03 Sensors, Code & Vision · Bench C. Line following on a proportional controller. A state machine for the mission. APEX adds colour thresholding, then a small on-device classifier.

WK 9–10 · Stage 3 Flight

Unit 04 Drone Systems · Flight Bay. Pre-flight checks, hover hold, and a supervised flight each.

WK 11–12 · Stage 4 Run

Mat Room. A full dry run at competition timing in week eleven, then the scored run in week twelve. Two attempts, best one counts.
