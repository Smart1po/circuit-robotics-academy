Straight answers

Does my child need to know how to code?

No. SPARK starts in a block editor, RELAY moves to text, APEX writes the whole control loop.

Do you enter students into competitions?

No. Our track is written and scored in-house. We are not affiliated with any organiser and do not register students for external events.

What do they take home?

Their logbook, their code and their build notes. Kits stay with us between terms; the deposit comes back when the kit does.

Is there a trial session?

One per family, per band, in week one or two. It is the same session everyone else is doing.

Is the soldering safe?

Safety glasses at Bench B whenever an iron is hot, fume extraction running, and SPARK students never hold the iron.
